Mushin Launcher is a home screen that gets you to the right app in one touch and nothing more. A short list of names replaces the grid of colourful icons. There are no feeds, no ads and no distractions.

Spaces are app sets for each part of your life:
• a normal space, a work space and up to 20 of your own;
• each space has its own app list, palette and background;
• long-press the background to switch.

Instant search:
• double-tap the background and the built-in keyboard is already on screen, with no system keyboard lag;
• finds apps by prefix, by substring, by the initials of their words, with typos, in the wrong keyboard layout and in transliteration;
• your recent apps for the current space sit on the right.

Swipe typing:
• trace a word over the invisible layout and Mushin recognises an installed app's name by the shape of the line, even if it is drawn sloppily or off to the side;
• if the drawing looks like several apps, Mushin shows them all;
• turn on Gesture input to draw shapes right on the home screen: Mushin learns your gestures, entirely on the device.

Make it yours:
• calm built-in backgrounds: solid colours and Japanese ink drawings in light and dark versions, or your own photo;
• five fonts, three text sizes, labels on the left, in the centre or on the right;
• swipes in four directions launch an app, open the notification shade or open search;
• rename or hide apps, show icons next to the names or no icons at all;
• five press-feedback styles and optional notification counts next to app names.

Privacy. Mushin has no internet access, no analytics and no ads. All data stays on your device, and Settings → System → Privacy erases it in one tap.

Languages: English, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch, Français, Bahasa Indonesia and Русский, with native keyboard layouts.

How to enable. After installing, open Mushin Launcher and agree to make it your home app. You can change this later in Settings → Apps → Default apps → Home app.
